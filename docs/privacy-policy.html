Privacy Policy

CTET & State TET Prep — last updated 14 August 2026

This page explains what information the CTET & State TET Prep app ("the app", "we") collects, why, and how you can control or delete it. If anything here is unclear, contact us at the email at the bottom of this page.

Information we collect

Account information

When you sign up (Google, email/password, or mobile number), we collect your name, email address and/or phone number, and profile photo (if you sign in with Google). If you fill in your profile, we also store your city, institution, designation, a short "about you" note, and which exam(s) you're preparing for.

Location

The app asks for location access only to auto-fill your city in your profile when you tap "Auto Fill" — it is not collected in the background and is not used for tracking.

Study activity

To make the app useful, we store the data you generate while using it: mock test attempts and scores, syllabus progress, notes, timetable entries, dictionary words you've saved, and bookmarks.

Push notification token

If you allow notifications, your device registers a token with us so we can send you reminders and updates. This token is tied to your account and removed if it stops working or you delete your account.

Analytics and crash reports

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (both Google services) to understand how the app is used and to catch crashes/bugs. This includes device type, app version, screens visited, and crash logs — it does not include the content of your notes or answers.

Advertising

The app shows ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may use a device advertising identifier to personalize ads — you can opt out of personalized ads in your device's system settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

How we use this information

  • To run your account and sync your progress across sessions
  • To show you relevant mock tests/PYQs for the exam(s) you selected
  • To send reminders and announcements (you can decline notification permission at any time)
  • To fix bugs and improve the app based on crash reports and usage patterns
  • To show ads that keep the app free

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. It is stored with our backend provider (Supabase) and processed by Google services we use directly in the app: Firebase (authentication bridge, push notifications, analytics, crash reporting) and AdMob (ads). Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data on our behalf.

Your choices

  • Edit or remove profile details anytime from Edit Profile in the app.
  • Turn off notifications from your device's app settings.
  • Delete your account from Edit Profile — this permanently removes your profile and account. Some anonymized usage/crash data already sent to Google's analytics services may not be individually deletable, as is standard for those services.

Children's privacy

This app is intended for teacher-eligibility-exam aspirants and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will change. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
